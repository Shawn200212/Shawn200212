## 关于我 · About

中央美术学院设计学院硕士在读（沉浸体验与游戏设计研究方向，2024–2027），本科毕业于央美数字媒体艺术专业（86.32/100，专业前 5%，优秀毕业设计）。以游戏引擎为主要创作媒介，横跨独立游戏、XR 开发、实时影像、具身智能与城市研究。当前研究方向：**三维 / XR 游戏开发与游戏中的强化学习**。

MA student in **Immersive Experience & Game Design** at the School of Design, Central Academy of Fine Arts (CAFA), Beijing (2024–2027); BFA in Digital Media Art at CAFA (2020–2024, top 5%, distinguished graduation project). I use game engines as my primary creative medium, working across indie games, XR development, realtime visuals, embodied AI and urban research. Current focus: **3D/XR game development and reinforcement learning in games**.
